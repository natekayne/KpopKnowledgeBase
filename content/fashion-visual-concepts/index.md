# Fashion & Visual Concepts

K-Pop visuals are a huge part of every comeback. Outfits, hair, makeup, album styling, and music video concepts can completely change the vibe of an era.

Some groups go cute, dark, futuristic, retro, or totally experimental. Fans usually remember the visuals almost as much as the song itself.
