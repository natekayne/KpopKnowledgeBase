# Fourth Generation of K-Pop
## Overview

Fourth-generation K-Pop is where the industry became even more global, competitive, and heavily driven by social media. This generation is usually connected to groups that debuted from around 2018 into the early 2020s. By this point, K-Pop already had a huge international audience because of the [[third-generation]], so newer groups were able to build global fanbases almost immediately instead of waiting years to expand outside Korea.

TikTok, YouTube Shorts, streaming, fancams, dance challenges, and online fan communities became a huge part of promotion. Album collecting also became even bigger, with multiple versions, photocards, fan-sign events, and preorder benefits becoming normal parts of a comeback.

## Best-Selling and Major Groups
- **Stray Kids** – Became one of the strongest physical album sellers of the generation and built a huge international fanbase. 
- **TXT** – Grew into one of the biggest fourth-gen boy groups with strong album sales and major international touring. 
- **ATEEZ** – Built a large global fandom and became especially known for intense performances and strong stage presence.
- **ENHYPEN** – Quickly became a major seller with a large international fanbase and strong popularity in Japan.
- **aespa** – Became one of the biggest fourth-gen girl groups with major album sales and a futuristic concept that stood out early in the generation.
- **IVE** – Became one of the strongest digital and physical performers among newer girl groups.
- **LE SSERAFIM** – Built a major global following through strong performances, social media, and successful releases.
- **NewJeans** – Had a huge impact on K-Pop trends with a more relaxed visual style and music that stood out from many traditional idol concepts. 
## Why This Generation Matters

Fourth gen feels like the first generation where global success was expected from the beginning. Groups did not have to slowly introduce themselves to international fans because the audience was already there. Companies started planning world tours, international promotions, English releases, and global partnerships much earlier in a group's career.
### Connection to Modern K-Pop

Fourth-gen groups continued the [[global-expansion]] started by earlier generations, but they also changed how K-Pop is marketed. Viral challenges, short-form videos, collectible albums, and constant online content became almost as important as traditional television promotions.

> Fourth-generation K-Pop grew up in an industry that was already global, so international fans became part of the plan from day one.
