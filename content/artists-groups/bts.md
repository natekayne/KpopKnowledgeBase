# BTS

BTS debuted in 2013 and became one of the biggest groups to come out of K-Pop. The group has seven members and is known for mixing pop, hip-hop, R&B, and other styles while also being involved in their music.

Their fandom, ARMY, became a huge part of their global success. BTS helped introduce even more people to K-Pop through international tours, streaming, social media, and major chart success.

## Why They Matter

BTS was a huge part of the [[kpop-history/third-generation|Third Generation]] and helped push the [[kpop-history/global-expansion|Global Expansion]] of K-Pop even further.

They are also a good example of how fandom culture and online communities can help a group grow way beyond South Korea.
