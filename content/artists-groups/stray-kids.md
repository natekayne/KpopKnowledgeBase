# Stray Kids

Stray Kids debuted in 2018 and became one of the biggest groups of the [[kpop-history/fourth-generation|Fourth Generation]]. They are known for loud, experimental music, strong performances, and having members involved in writing and producing a lot of their songs.

Their fandom, STAY, is huge internationally, and the group has built a strong reputation for intense concerts and high-energy stages.

## Why They Matter

Stray Kids helped show how fast fourth-gen groups could build global fanbases. Their album sales, tours, and online popularity made them one of the biggest examples of modern K-Pop's international growth.
