# TWICE

TWICE debuted in 2015 and became one of the biggest girl groups of the [[kpop-history/third-generation|Third Generation]]. They are known for catchy songs, colorful concepts, strong choreography, and a huge fandom called ONCE.

Their music started with brighter concepts and later grew into a more mature style while still keeping that very recognizable TWICE sound.

## Why They Matter

TWICE became massive in both South Korea and Japan and later grew even more internationally. They helped show how strong a girl group could be in album sales, touring, and long-term fandom support.
