# SEVENTEEN

SEVENTEEN debuted in 2015 and became one of the biggest groups of the [[kpop-history/third-generation|Third Generation]]. They are known for having a lot of members, super clean synchronization, and being heavily involved in their own music and performances.

Their fandom is called CARAT, and the group has built a huge following through strong albums, variety content, and really solid live stages.

## Why They Matter

SEVENTEEN is a good example of a self-producing idol group. Members take part in songwriting, choreography, and performance planning, which helped them stand out.
