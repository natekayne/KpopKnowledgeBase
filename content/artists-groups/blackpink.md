# BLACKPINK

BLACKPINK debuted in 2016 and became one of the biggest girl groups in K-Pop. The group has four members: Jisoo, Jennie, Rosé, and Lisa.

Their music mixes pop, hip-hop, EDM, and strong performance concepts. They are also known for fashion, huge music videos, and major international tours.

## Why They Matter

BLACKPINK helped push K-Pop girl groups even further into the global mainstream. Their fandom, BLINK, is massive worldwide, and the group became a major part of the [[kpop-history/third-generation|Third Generation]].

Their concerts are also known for huge lightstick crowds and the iconic pink ocean.
