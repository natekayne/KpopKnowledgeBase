# aespa

aespa debuted in 2020 and became one of the biggest groups of the [[kpop-history/fourth-generation|Fourth Generation]]. They are known for futuristic concepts, strong vocals, electronic production, and visuals that feel very high-tech.

Their fandom is called MY, and the group quickly built a big international following.

## Why They Matter

aespa helped make virtual concepts and futuristic storytelling feel more normal in K-Pop. Their music and visuals also helped define part of the fourth-gen sound.
