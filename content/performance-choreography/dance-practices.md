# Dance Practices

Dance practice videos are a huge part of K-Pop because they let fans actually see the choreography without all the stage effects, camera cuts, and outfits getting in the way.

A lot of fans use them to learn dances, compare formations, or just appreciate how synchronized a group is. Some dance practices even become almost as popular as the official performance videos.

## Why It Matters

Choreography is one of the biggest parts of K-Pop performance. Dance practice videos make that side of the genre way easier to understand and enjoy.
