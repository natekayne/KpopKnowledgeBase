# Lightsticks & Fandom Culture

K-Pop fandom culture is honestly one of the biggest things that makes the genre feel different. Fans do way more than just listen to the music. They collect albums, trade photocards, learn fan chants, stream comebacks, and show up to concerts with official lightsticks.

Lightsticks are especially iconic because each group usually has its own design and colors. When thousands of fans use them at the same time, the whole arena can turn into a giant light ocean.

## Why It Matters

Fandoms help groups build strong communities and stay connected with fans around the world. The fan experience is a huge part of modern K-Pop.
