# Photocards & Album Collecting

Collecting is a huge part of K-Pop fandom. Albums usually come with extras like photocards, posters, stickers, and photo books, which makes buying a physical album feel way more fun than just getting the music.

Photocards are probably the most iconic part. Fans trade and collect cards of their favorite members. Some rare cards can become super hard to find.

## Why It Matters

Album collecting helps fans feel more connected to a comeback and gives each era its own little collection moment. It also helps explain why physical albums are still such a big deal in K-Pop.
